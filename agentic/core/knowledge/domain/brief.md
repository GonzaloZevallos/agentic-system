# Brief

## Problem and who has it

Describe the problem and who has it: who feels this pain today, how often, and what
they do instead of the product. One paragraph is enough.

## Jobs

Job stories, the same format PM's definitions use: "When [situation], I want
[motivation], so I can [expected outcome]." One per job the product serves, with the
criterion the user themselves judges it by — that criterion is the success metric;
there is no separate section for it.

- When [situation], I want [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].

## Constraints

What bounds the solution: scale (orders of magnitude, not a single number that will
be stale in a month), anything regulatory, hard deadlines, and integrations the
product must honour because something outside it already depends on them.

- Scale: [orders of magnitude the product must hold up to].
- Regulatory: [what applies, or "none"].
- Deadlines: [hard dates, or "none"].
- Integrations: [systems the product must talk to, and why they are mandatory].

## Out of scope

What the product deliberately does not do, so nobody builds it by accident.
